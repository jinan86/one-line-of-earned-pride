## The entry

> After eleven winters polishing the lens by hand, the keeper watched his beam cut the fog — and far below, a ship answered with its horn.

A lighthouse keeper, eleven winters of hand-polishing the lens, and one foggy night when the beam finally cuts through and a ship answers with its horn. The pride is never named — it lives in the long effort and in the payoff. No trophy, no podium, no graduation.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes that single deterministic line to `/output/entry.txt`. Running it twice produces byte-identical output: 136 characters, UTF-8, one line.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
